A few useful tips

Keep recyclables loose, clean and empty

Empty containers, remove food residue and place items directly in the bin, not inside a bag.

Separate clean and greasy packaging

For pizza boxes, the existing item guide says to separate clean cardboard from the food-soiled base.

Keep batteries out of household bins

The item guide directs household batteries to a dedicated battery drop-off. Check local drop-off options before travelling.

Yellow-lid bin

Drink bottles, water bottles and similar plastic containers.

Eligible drink containers can also be returned for a 10c refund through Return and Earn.

How to do it

1 Empty the bottle (no liquid remaining).
2 Keep the lid on (unless your council asks to remove it).
3 No need to crush - a quick rinse is fine.
4 Put it in loose, not inside a plastic bag.
