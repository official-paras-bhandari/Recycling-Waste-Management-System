Bin collection rules

Red lid · General waste Weekly (Every Thursday) Non-recyclable domestic items, bagged waste, broken glass (wrapped).
Yellow lid · Recycling Fortnightly (Every 2nd Thursday) Cardboard, paper, rigid plastics (#1–5), glass jars & bottles, tins. Rinse clean & leave lids loose.
Green lid · Organics & FOGO Fortnightly (Every 2nd Thursday) Food scraps, fruit peels, lawn clippings, leaves, pruned branches. No plastic bags or packaging!

Kerbside placement guidelines

  • 1 Place bins out before 7:00 am on collection day.
  • 2 Leave at least 50 cm between bins and clear of parked vehicles.
  • 3 Keep lids fully closed so waste is not blown away or scavenged.
  • 4 Position wheels towards your house and handles facing you.
